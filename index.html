Victorian Public Transport

This dashboard explores public transport patronage in Victoria. It highlights station entries, daily travel patterns, and monthly trends across different transport modes to show how people use the city’s public transport network.

Map & Top Stations Overview

Station patronage concentrates heavily in the inner city, with the ten busiest stops spanning the City Loop and key suburban gateways.

Most of the ten busiest stations act as multi-line interchanges, so transfer hubs concentrate much of the demand.

Patronage Heatmap (FY2024–25)

Heatmap reveals weekday peaks and a clear winter lull.

Allows for quick identification of patterns across different days and months. Allowing for governing bodies to add or remove services accordingly.

Monthly Patronage Trends & Mode Contribution

Monthly lines show long-term trends and pandemic recovery; the stacked view highlights how modes share the task.

Sources: PTV Public Transport Stops & Annual metropolitan train station patronage (CSV). Built with Vega-Lite.
